Documented Contradiction

Official Record Discrepancy

The KCSO website stated Norris joined as a lateral deputy in 2012. His LASD retirement was publicly recorded as March 2014. A two-year gap in the official account, confirmed through public employment records and press reporting.

Pattern of Failure

4 Consecutive ISA Failures

Kootenai County Jail has failed Idaho Sheriff's Association certification for four straight years. Documented through state inspection records, Wolfinger's written responses, and correspondence with the ISA. The jail has operated without valid certification since at least 2022.

Budget Records

5 Years of Overtime Overruns

KCSO overtime has exceeded its approved budget every fiscal year for five consecutive years. Sourced directly from county budget resolutions. Total budget grew 55% between FY2021 and FY2026, confirmed through public financial records.

Retaliation — Documented

Deputization Revocations

Sheriff Norris revoked the deputy status of the CDA Police Chief, a Captain who was actively investigating Norris, and the Rathdrum Police Chief. Timeline and basis documented from public statements, press coverage, and official records. Case went nationally covered in February 2025.

In-Custody Deaths

Multiple Deaths — Gap in Reporting

Multiple in-custody deaths documented at Kootenai County Jail from 2024 to 2025. Idaho did not require jails to report deaths to the state during this period, making any official count almost certainly incomplete. Timelines built from coroner records, press reports, and family accounts.

Contract Analysis

Billing vs. Cost Discrepancies

Contract billing records obtained via public records request show discrepancies between what was publicly stated about policing contract costs and what the actual county expenditure records show. Figures documented and sourced to the original contract and county financial records.
